# Power board PCB layout (v2)

Board: 100 x 86 mm, 2 layers, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper. Origin at lower-left,
Y grows upward. Placement and outline are generated by `place_pcb.py`;
routing by `route_pcb.py` (autoroute + GND pours), both reproducible from
the scripts.

## Stackup

| Layer | Use |
|---|---|
| TOP | signal + power tracks, GND pour |
| BOTTOM | signal tracks, GND pour |
| Board outline | closed polyline on layer 11, 100 x 86 mm |

## Zones

```text
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| J1 XT30 in   F1 blade fuse   SW1 main switch   Q1 AOD409  R10 D5 C18 |  ~y 73
| (battery)                                                             |
|  D1 TVS   C1 470u   C12   C21   RSH 0.01R                            |  ~y 58
|  FB1  U1 MP2315#1   C2/C3  C13 R11  C15 R13          U2 #2 mirror     |  ~y 48
|  L1   C4 C5 C6  R1 R2 C19  FB3                      L2 ... FB4        |  ~y 32
|  J3 5V1 out   U4 INA219 U3 ME6211 C22-24   SW2 motor  J5 XT30 out    |  ~y 24
|  J4 5V2 out   J7 I2C     LEDs D2-D4 + R7-R9      TP1-TP4             |  ~y 8
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

- Left edge: J1 battery in (top), J3 5V1 out, J4 5V2 out, J7 I2C.
- Right edge: J5 XT30 motor out; SW2 (motor cut) just left of it.
- Top strip: fuse and switches carry the full battery current — traces
  between J1/F1/SW1/Q1/RSH are the highest-current paths on the board.
- Mounting: 4 x 3.2 mm NPTH at the corners (H0-H3).

## Trace widths (final, before Gerber export)

The autorouter runs everything at 10 mil. Before exporting, re-route the
power section manually in the GUI:

| Net(s) | Target width | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| VBAT_BMS, VBAT_FUSED, VBAT_SW, VBAT_RAIL, VBAT_LOAD, VBAT_MOTOR | >= 3 mm or copper pours | 10 A class motor rail |
| 5V1, 5V1_OUT, 5V2, 5V2_OUT | >= 0.8 mm | 3 A class rails |
| SW5_x / BST_x / FB_x | 0.25 mm | signal |

GND is poured on both layers; keep the motor return path away from the
logic area (star grounding near the battery input, DECISIONS.md D10).

## Rules

- Track/copper clearance: default (the 1 oz rule), verified by
  `run_drc.py` — the automated layout passes with 0 violations.
- VBAT bulk caps (C1) and the shunt (RSH) sit directly in the battery
  path; keep the buck input caps (C2/C3, C7/C8) within a few mm of the
  MP2315 IN pins.
- The SW node (MP2315 pin 3 -> inductor) is the noisy node: keep the
  SW-L-Cout loop small.
- INA219 IN+/IN- sense lines land on the shunt pads (Kelvin-ish, short
  tracks); keep them away from the motor rail.
